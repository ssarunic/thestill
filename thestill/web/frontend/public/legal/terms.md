# Terms of Service

These terms apply to thestill, a service that summarises podcast episodes and makes short, attributed quotes from them searchable.

## What thestill does

thestill reads publicly available podcast feeds, transcribes episodes with speech recognition and writes summaries with a language model. Signed-out visitors can read summaries and short quotes; signed-in users can also read full transcripts, follow shows and receive briefings.

## Accuracy

Summaries and quotes are produced automatically and can be wrong, including who said something. Every quote links to its episode and minute so you can check it against the original audio. Do not rely on thestill as a record of what anyone said.

## Podcasts belong to their publishers

Shows, artwork and audio belong to their publishers. thestill links to the publisher's own pages on Apple Podcasts, Spotify, YouTube and the show's feed. Publishers can ask for a show to be removed from public pages at any time.

## People

If you are named on a person page and want it removed, contact us and we will hide it.

## Acceptable use

Do not scrape the site at volume, resell its content, or use it to train models without permission.
