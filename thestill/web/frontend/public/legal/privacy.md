# Privacy Policy

## Signed-out visitors

Reading public pages sets no cookies and runs no third-party trackers. Like any web server, ours keeps short-lived request logs for security and rate limiting.

To show the podcast chart for your country, we look up the country of your IP address with ipinfo.io. We keep the answer for a day and do not store your address with it.

## Signed-in users

Signing in with Google gives thestill your name, email address and profile picture. We use them to run your account: the shows you follow, your inbox, briefings and settings. On your first sign-in we look up your country from your IP address (ipinfo.io) to pick your default chart; you can change it in Settings. We do not sell your data or show you advertising.

## Data you can remove

You can delete your account and its data at any time by contacting us.

## Contact

Questions about privacy go to the contact address on the project's GitHub page.
